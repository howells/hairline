PairedRungs lays its categories out as rows when the chart is too narrow for its columns: each row carries its name and both values on one line, the current period as rungs along a track and the earlier period hatched beneath, on one scale, and the chart grows taller instead of crowding its labels. PairedRungs also takes `width` and `initialWidth`, so it renders complete on the server like the packaged charts.
