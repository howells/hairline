Slope takes `alert` ("rise", "fall" or "none"), as DumbbellQueue does: which direction of change is bad news. Its biggest change is drawn in the alert colour and the other direction's biggest in the hero colour. It defaults to "rise", which is how Slope already drew, so set "fall" for a measure where rising is good, such as visits or revenue.
