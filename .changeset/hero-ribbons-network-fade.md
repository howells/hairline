A hero group stands against its context. PlumbScatter, HairlineLine, JitterStrip and DotCascade take `hero`, a list of the names (or dates, or columns) to single out, and `context`, `"grey"` or `"opacity"`, for how the rest recedes; hero marks sit on a paper ring above the context. PlumbScatter and HairlineLine's `emphasis`, JitterStrip's `mark` and DotCascade's `highlight` are gone, replaced by `hero`. PlumbScatter takes `heroLabel` to name the group directly with a leader. ChordRing, AggregateSankey and HourglassStream fill each ribbon with one gradient of the ink, dark at its source. ForceGraph, ForceGalaxy and both CircularGraphs fade out from the selected node by links, name it with its reach, and take `selected` to open on a node; tiny nodes are found by their nearest neighbour, so a finger can hit them.
