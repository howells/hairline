# Chart audit

Audited 2026-11-04 against `docs/REFINE.md` and the audit checklist, from real renders of `main` at 0.4.1: 107 charts, each at 1280 and 390 wide in Pink, Paper and Dark, plus hover at 1280 in Pink and Dark (deviceScaleFactor 2, reduced motion). Each link is a contact sheet for that chart. Top row: Pink 1280, Dark 1280, Pink hover, Dark hover. Bottom row: Pink 390, Paper 390, Dark 390, Paper 1280.

**1 broken, 64 wrong, 175 rough.** No console errors or warnings on any chart. Clean: F5, F11, D3, D20.

Method: a DOM probe measured text overlaps, odd stroke widths, alert use, caps and mono, and ragged numbers. Two review passes then judged every chart's shots by eye. Bounding-box overlaps on rotated labels and the hidden data tables were false positives and are left out.
